Urbz District Editor

Load your own dump of The Urbz: Sims in the City (GBA, BOCE).

Everything runs in this page; the ROM never leaves your machine. Pick a district, choose a layer or Collision, click a piece on the right and paint it onto the map. Right-click a map cell to pick up what is there. Export writes a UPS patch you can apply with any patcher (or the bundled gba_patchfile.py).

Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, drag to paint. Collision colours: red blocks, green is pavement the Sim walks on, blue is a special edge or step, unpainted is open.
